# Business Assumptions

- Late deliveries create delay handling cost and can increase customer-service workload.
- Stockouts create lost-sales exposure based on shipped quantity and unit price.
- Holding cost is configurable as an annual percentage of inventory value.
- Ordering cost is configurable as a fixed cost per replenishment order.
- Service level is configurable and converted to a safety-stock z-score.
- Carrier capacity is estimated from observed shipment volumes with a configurable buffer.
- Synthetic data includes peak-season delay pressure, supplier quality variation, route congestion, weather severity, expedited cost premiums, and intermittent demand.
